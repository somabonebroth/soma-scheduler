Rename Ingredient Lots

Lots keep the name they were received under. When a recipe ingredient is renamed (e.g. "Carrots" → "Organic Carrots"), the old lots no longer match any recipe and show as zero stock. This renames every lot of the old name, past ones included, so recipes, stock and history agree. Each lot keeps a note of its original name. Past batch records are not changed.
