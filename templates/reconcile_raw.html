Reconcile Raw Material Stock

Rebuilds every raw-material lot's remaining balance from scratch: each lot is reset to its received quantity, then every completed production run is replayed in date order, drawing only from lots that existed when that batch was made. This corrects balances that were drained by the old date-blind deduction (e.g. a delivery consumed by a run that predated it).

Preview first. Nothing is written until you click Apply. Manual Edit Qty adjustments are recomputed away by this rebuild — any lots affected are listed below so you can re-enter them afterward.
