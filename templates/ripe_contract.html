1. Parties & Term

This Wholesale Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between SOMA BONE BROTH CO LTD. (the "Supplier") and RIPE NUTRITION INC. (the "Purchaser"). The Agreement is effective from {{ effective_date }} and remains in force until {{ expiry_date }}, after which it may be renewed by mutual written agreement on revised pricing.

During the term, the prices and terms set out below are fixed; Supplier may not unilaterally change them.

2. Order Minimums

One case contains {{ units_per_case }} units. Minimums depend on the delivery method:

  • Delivery (Coco Market, Woodville, Summerhill Warehouse): minimum {{ min_ss_cases }} cases of Shelf-Stable (SS) per order. There is no minimum for Frozen (FZ) or Back Bar (BB) products — these may be added in any quantity.
  • Pickup (Soma facility, 676 Pape Ave): no minimum. Any combination and quantity of products may be ordered for pickup.

5. Cancellation & Refunds

  • Orders not yet invoiced: cancelled instantly with no charge.
  • Orders with a Stripe invoice sent but unpaid: invoice is voided automatically on cancellation.
  • Orders that have been paid: refund must be issued through the Stripe Dashboard before the order can be marked cancelled in the portal.

6. Order Edits

Ripe may edit any order while it is still pending Soma's approval. Once Soma approves an order, edits are no longer permitted — changes after approval require cancellation and re-submission.

7. Missed Pickup & Re-packing Fee

If Ripe fails to collect a pickup order on the agreed date, Soma may charge a flat $100 re-packing fee invoiced separately via Stripe, due on receipt.
