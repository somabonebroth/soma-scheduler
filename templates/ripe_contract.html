1. Parties & Term

This Wholesale Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between SOMA BONE BROTH CO LTD. (the "Supplier") and RIPE NUTRITION INC. (the "Purchaser"). The Agreement is effective from {{ effective_date }} and remains in force until {{ expiry_date }}, after which it may be renewed by mutual written agreement on revised pricing.

During the term, the prices and terms set out below are fixed; Supplier may not unilaterally change them.

2. Order Minimums

One case contains {{ units_per_case }} units. Minimums depend on the delivery method:

  • Delivery (Coco Market, Woodville, Summerhill Warehouse): minimum {{ min_ss_cases }} cases of Shelf-Stable (SS) per order. Frozen (FZ) and Back Bar (BB) products may be added in any quantity on top of the SS minimum.

Just-In-Time (JIT) ordering — FZ and BB formats: FZ and BB products are not warehoused as standing inventory and are produced to order. The following lead times apply when ordering FZ or BB products with insufficient stock on hand:

  • Orders of {{ fzbb_threshold if fzbb_threshold is defined else 8 }} cases or fewer: minimum {{ fzbb_small_lead if fzbb_small_lead is defined else 3 }} business days notice before requested date.
  • Orders of {{ fzbb_threshold if fzbb_threshold is defined else 8 }}+ cases: minimum {{ fzbb_large_lead if fzbb_large_lead is defined else 7 }} business days notice before requested date.

Lead time requirements are enforced at order approval. Orders submitted without sufficient notice will be declined and must be re-submitted with a later requested date.

5. Cancellation & Store Credit

Either party may cancel an order at any point before fulfillment without penalty. Cancellation rules:

  • Orders not yet invoiced: cancelled instantly with no charge.
  • Orders with a Stripe invoice sent but unpaid: invoice is voided automatically on cancellation.
  • Orders that have been paid: payment is converted to store credit applicable against future Soma wholesale orders. Store credit does not expire during the term of this Agreement.

6. Order Edits

Ripe may edit any order while it is still pending Soma's approval. Saving changes replaces the previous version. Once Soma approves an order, edits are no longer permitted — order changes after approval require a cancellation and re-submission.

7. Missed Pickup & Re-packing Fee

Pickup orders at Soma's facility require Ripe to collect on the scheduled date. If Ripe fails to pick up on the agreed date, Soma may charge a flat $100 re-packing fee to cover the cost of breaking down and re-preparing the order. The fee is invoiced separately through Stripe and is due immediately on receipt.

Each missed pickup may incur its own re-packing fee. When Soma charges a fee, a new pickup date is scheduled jointly with Ripe.
