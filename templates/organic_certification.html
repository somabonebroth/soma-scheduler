Audit Pack

One PDF to hand an inspector: the recipes that ran, the CCP plan, a completed checklist as the worked example, and every batch in the period traced from supplier lot to buyer.

An organic audit asks four things. Each one has a page that answers it.

The four questions
1📋Are your suppliers' certificates current?The card below. Expiry dates and certificates are kept on Buyers & Suppliers. 2📦Show me receiving records with lot numbersEvery delivery with supplier, LOT# and invoice, on the Sales & Receiving Record.
3🔎Trace one lot, forward or backA supplier LOT# forward to batches, finished goods and sales, or an FG LOT# back to its raw lots. 4📊Does organic in equal organic out?Mass Balance: Opening + Received − Consumed against what is on the shelf, for the year.
Keep the numbers honest

Mass Balance is only as good as the stock figures behind it. Count now and then; organic finished goods are counted one LOT at a time.

🔍Count Finished GoodsPhysical count against the books. This is the count that covers your certified product. 🔍Count Raw MaterialsPhysical count of raw materials against the books. 📜Stock Count HistoryPast counts, with dates and what changed. 📑DocumentsYour own organic certificate and compliance paperwork.

Repair tools, only if the numbers look wrong: Rebuild raw balances (replays every completed batch; rewrites the raw books) · Check finished goods for drift (read-only, lot by lot; the zero-day reset is behind it).
