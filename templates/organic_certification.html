Before an audit — work through in order

Each step builds on the one before it. Steps 1 and 3 have a raw-material side and a finished-goods side — do both. Finished goods are the certified product that actually leaves the building, so an audit that only proves the raw side proves half the chain.

1♻Reconcile Raw MaterialsMake the books accurate first — replay every completed production run so raw-material balances match what was actually used. 1♻Reconcile Finished GoodsThe same for the FG side — find where recorded stock has drifted from produced minus sold, and what explains each gap.
2📊Mass BalanceCheck the numbers add up, both sides at once — Opening + Received − Consumed should equal what's on the shelf. Investigate any gap.
3🔍Count Raw MaterialsConfirm it for real — physically count raw materials and compare them to the books. 3🔍Count Finished GoodsPhysically count finished goods and compare them to the books. This is the count that covers your certified product.

Stock Exceptions —

Completed batches recorded as produced with less raw material on file than the recipe required. These are historical markers, not open problems — they do not clear, and a non-zero count is not itself a fault. The day each one happened also raises it on that day's Management Report.
