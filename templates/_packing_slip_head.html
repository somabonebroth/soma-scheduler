{# Shared block for the three HTML packing slips (ripe / ripe-retail / SBBC retail). Edit here, never fork it per slip. The slips print on 4in x 6in THERMAL labels. Two consequences drive every rule below: 1. The sheet is laid out AT 4in wide, on screen and on paper. The old slips were designed 800px wide for letter and the label printer shrank them to fit (~47%), which is what made the type unreadable. 2. Thermal heads print black or nothing (~203dpi). Grey text and tinted fills dither into mush, so everything is pure black on white: weight and rules carry the hierarchy, not colour. Sizes are in pt/in so they mean the same thing on paper as they say here. A long order simply runs onto a second label: rows never split, and the table head (which carries the order number) repeats on every label. #}
