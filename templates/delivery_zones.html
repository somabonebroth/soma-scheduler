How a code is matched. The longest matching pattern wins, not the first one listed: a full 6-character pattern beats a 5-character prefix beats a 3-character prefix. So Zone 1 claims specific M5J sub-codes, Zone 2’s bare M5J* takes the rest of M5J, and Zone 3’s L* / M* only catch what Zones 1 and 2 left. Zones are edited in delivery_zones.json in the repo and redeployed.
